How it works

✔

Start Subscription

  • Send OK to 9000 via SMS.
  • You will receive a welcome message from Arada Games confirming your subscription.
📝

Get Access Details

  • Arada Games will send you:
    • A game link: Arada-Games.et
    • A temporary password
  • Use your phone number as the username and the password sent via SMS to log in.
  • You can change your password after logging in.
🎮

Explore the Platform

  • Once logged in, you'll find a variety of single and multiplayer games.
  • Each game includes:
    • How to Play instructions
    • Game Rules
    • Help & Support Info
🏆

Leaderboard & Profile

  • Check the leaderboard to see top players and current rankings.
  • Update your profile and set a nickname — your nickname will be shown on the leaderboard.
💰

Earn Free Coins

  • Share the game with your friends using your referral link or number.
  • When your referrals join and play, you'll earn free coins.
⏳

Daily Access & Coins

  • 1 coin = 1 day of access to the platform.
  • The platform is subscription-based:
    • 2 Birr is deducted from your airtime balance daily
🎁

Free Trial & Unsubscribe

  • On your first subscription, you'll get a 3-day free trial.
  • If you unsubscribe and resubscribe later, you'll be charged immediately — no second trial.
  • To unsubscribe, send STOP to 9000.
  • You'll receive a confirmation SMS for your cancellation.
